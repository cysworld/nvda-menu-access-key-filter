Reading of runs 2 and 3:
- With Microsoft Pinyin the IME works in an ordinary text box (ren + space gives U+4EBA) in this window.
- While the menu bar is active, "ren" reaches the menu as the raw letter r (WM_MENUCHAR U+0072) with no IME composition
  message. The result is identical to the baseline without an IME (run 3). Windows only reports the first
  unmatched character, so e and n do not appear in either run.
- A Latin mnemonic (t) still works with the IME in either mode.
Limits: virtual-key events injected with SendInput rather than a physical keyboard, one IME (Microsoft Pinyin), one
menu implementation (native HMENU), one Windows build. Japanese, Korean and Bopomofo IMEs were not available.
